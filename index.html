PhD Student at Harvard University

Jin Young (Rachel) Shin

I study how early environments can shape children’s development and functioning with a focus on social-emotional development. This includes early environments, adversity, parent–child relationships, and parental mental health.

I hope to understand the mechanisms that put children at developmental risk and those that support resilience, and how interventions in homes and educational settings can strengthen protective pathways. I bring training in developmental psychology, clinical science, and neuroscience. At Harvard, I am advised by Dr. Stephanie Jones.
